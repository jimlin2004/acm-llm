## Prompt

An ideal inverting op-amp amplifier has Rin = 10 kohm and Rf = 470 kohm. What is the closed-loop voltage gain in dB, and what is the sign of the gain?

## Checks

- ✅ `number_near` — 33.4±2.0% (found [33.44, 33.44])
- ✅ `contains_any` — any of ['invert|negative|-\\s?47|180']

## Expected (manual bar)

|A| = 47 -> 33.4 dB, and it must note the inversion.

## Reply



For an ideal inverting op-amp amplifier, the closed-loop voltage gain is:

$$A_v = -\frac{R_f}{R_{in}}$$

Substituting your values:
$$A_v = -\frac{470\ \text{k}\Omega}{10\ \text{k}\Omega} = -47$$

**Sign of the gain:** Negative (the output signal is 180° out of phase with the input, as expected for an inverting configuration).

**Gain in dB:** Voltage gain in decibels is calculated from the magnitude:
$$A_{v(\text{dB})} = 20 \log_{10}(|A_v|) = 20 \log_{10}(47) \approx 33.44\ \text{dB}$$

**Summary:**
- Closed-loop voltage gain: **33.44 dB** (magnitude)
- Sign: **Negative** (−47 V/V linear gain)
