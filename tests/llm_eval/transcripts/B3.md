**Bandwidth Assessment**

The errors (`Error: no such vector as v(out).`) are caused by a node naming mismatch: your circuit defines the output node as `vout`, but the measurement commands are searching for `v(out)`. Because of this, the simulator could not automatically extract the 3 dB bandwidth.

However, the bandwidth can be calculated directly from the component values:
$$f_{3dB} = \frac{1}{2\pi RC} = \frac{1}{2\pi \times 10^4 \times 10^{-6}} \approx 15.92 \text{ Hz}$$

This cutoff frequency falls well within your simulated range (1 Hz – 100 kHz), so the simulation would have captured it correctly once the node names are aligned.

**Recommendations**

3. **Add `.end`:** Append a `.end` directive to the bottom of the netlist. While ngspice often tolerates its absence, it is standard practice and prevents unexpected parsing issues.
